FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most often from engineering and security leaders.

What kind of company is NYCConsult?

NYCConsult is a technology consulting company with more than 20 years in business. We work through a team of specialists across cloud architecture, platform engineering, DevSecOps, Kubernetes, security, compliance, observability, automation, data, and cloud optimization. Engagements are staffed based on the scope, timeline, and technologies involved.

What cloud platforms does NYCConsult support?

NYCConsult supports AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP), including cloud architecture, landing zones, migration, Kubernetes, security, and cost optimization.

Can NYCConsult help plan or deliver a cloud migration?

Yes. NYCConsult can assess workloads, define a migration roadmap, design secure cloud foundations, and support migration and modernization across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.

Can you help with compliance documentation (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001)?

Yes. We provide readiness assessments, control mappings, templates, evidence workflows, and implementation guidance to make compliance repeatable and less disruptive.

How do you handle security in CI/CD?

We implement a practical shift-left model: secrets scanning, SAST/DAST, SBOM, IaC policy checks, and secure approvals—balanced to keep delivery velocity high.

Do you offer fixed-scope engagements?

Yes. We can structure work as fixed-scope milestones (e.g., landing zone build, platform MVP) or as a retainer for ongoing platform and security improvements.
