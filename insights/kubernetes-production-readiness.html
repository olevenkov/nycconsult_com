Kubernetes production readiness checklist

Production readiness is more than a healthy control plane. Review how clusters are secured, workloads are deployed, failures are detected, and teams respond to incidents.

Cluster lifecycle and access

  • Document supported versions, upgrade cadence, and rollback or recovery plans.
  • Restrict control-plane access and use workload-specific identity where supported.
  • Define cluster separation and tenancy based on risk and operational needs.
  • Review quotas, capacity planning, autoscaling limits, and resource requests.

Workload security

Set expectations for image provenance, patching, least privilege, secret handling, network policy, admission controls, and runtime visibility. Make security requirements testable in the delivery path when practical.

Deployment and recovery

  • Use health checks and rollout strategies that fit each service.
  • Test rollback procedures and verify database or stateful workload recovery.
  • Define how configuration and secrets move between environments.
  • Make release ownership and emergency changes explicit.

Observability and service reliability

Collect useful metrics, logs, and traces while managing cost and sensitive data. Create alerts for actionable symptoms, define service-level indicators and objectives where appropriate, and connect alerts to owned runbooks.

Operational readiness

Identify who supports clusters and workloads, how incidents are escalated, and which recurring maintenance tasks are required. Validate the checklist with a representative service and a realistic failure scenario.
