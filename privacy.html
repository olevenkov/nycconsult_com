Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains the information handled when you use nycconsult.com and how optional Google Analytics works.

Effective November 26, 2026.

Information you submit

If you use the contact form, NYCConsult receives your name, work email, message, and the company name if you choose to provide it. The form sends this information to the website's contact service, which uses AWS API Gateway, Lambda, and Simple Email Service (SES) to deliver inquiries for a response.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, health information, or other sensitive data through the contact form.

Optional website analytics

Google Analytics 4 is off by default on this website. It loads only if you choose “Accept analytics” in the consent notice. You can reject analytics or change your choice later using “Privacy settings” in the page footer.

If enabled, analytics may receive information about page views, browser and device, and site interactions. Google Analytics may also automatically measure interactions such as scrolling, depending on the property's settings. NYCConsult sends a successful contact-form event and a Calendly-click event, but does not include form names, email addresses, company names, or message content in those analytics events. Google may use cookies or similar technologies when analytics is enabled; Google's own privacy terms apply to its processing.

The consent choice is saved in your browser's local storage. The site also loads Google Fonts, which may send technical request information to Google when a page is viewed.

Third-party services

If you choose to book a meeting, the Calendly link opens Calendly, which handles that booking under its own privacy terms. Information submitted through this site is also processed by the service providers used to deliver the contact form and, only after analytics consent, Google Analytics.

Use, retention, and requests

Contact information is used to review and respond to your inquiry and, where applicable, manage a business relationship. NYCConsult does not currently publish a fixed retention period. Inquiry records are retained based on the time needed to handle the request and related business or legal requirements.

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, email info@nycconsult.com. Requests are handled subject to applicable requirements and records NYCConsult is required to maintain.
