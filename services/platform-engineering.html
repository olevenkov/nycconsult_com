Platform engineering

Make the secure, supported path easier for delivery teams

NYCConsult helps engineering organizations design internal platforms that provide reusable patterns and self-service capabilities while preserving security, governance, and operational ownership.

Build around developer and operator needs

Platform engineering starts with the workflows teams need to perform, the constraints they face, and the capabilities platform owners can reliably support. A focused platform roadmap avoids building a portal or abstraction that does not remove a real source of friction.

  • Map common paths from repository creation through deployment and operation.
  • Identify repeated infrastructure, security, and release tasks.
  • Define ownership, support boundaries, and platform service expectations.
  • Prioritize platform capabilities with feedback from users.

Platform capabilities

  • Golden paths, service templates, and documented engineering standards.
  • Reusable Terraform modules and secure self-service provisioning.
  • CI/CD workflows, GitOps patterns, and deployment guardrails.
  • Developer documentation, service ownership, and operational handoffs.

Adopt incrementally

Start with a small number of high-value workflows, validate them with delivery teams, then expand based on adoption and operational feedback. Platform measures should reflect user experience and delivery flow—not just the number of tools or features deployed.
