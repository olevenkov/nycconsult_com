DevSecOps consulting

Build security and evidence into everyday delivery

NYCConsult helps teams integrate security checks, infrastructure guardrails, and compliance evidence into software delivery and cloud operations without treating a scanner or a document as a complete security program.

Connect controls to engineering workflows

Effective DevSecOps begins with an understanding of the systems, threat model, delivery workflow, and obligations that matter to the organization. Controls should be actionable, assigned to owners, and tuned to reduce avoidable noise.

  • Review application and infrastructure delivery paths.
  • Prioritize secrets scanning, SAST, dependency checks, and IaC policy checks.
  • Define software bill of materials (SBOM) and release evidence practices.
  • Establish exception, remediation, and secure approval workflows.

Compliance readiness support

For frameworks such as SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, and ISO 27001, support can include readiness gap reviews, control mapping, evidence workflows, and implementation guidance. Readiness support is not a certification, audit opinion, or substitute for qualified legal and audit advice.

Make evidence repeatable

Where practical, connect technical controls to durable evidence sources such as change records, access reviews, configuration checks, and monitoring. Document control ownership and the process for investigating exceptions.

Independent cybersecurity assessment

For a broader security evaluation or an authorized penetration test, see our cybersecurity assessment service. Testing is performed only with written authorization and an agreed scope.
