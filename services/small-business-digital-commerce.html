Small business digital commerce

Connect your website, store, inventory, and ad accounts

NYCConsult helps small businesses design and build websites and online stores, connect inventory applications within an agreed scope, and improve the security of Google Ads and Meta Ads accounts.

Website and online store design

Start with the business workflow: what customers need to find, how products or services are presented, and how inquiries, bookings, or purchases are handled. The site structure, content, and integrations are planned around the actual business requirements.

  • Website and e-commerce store design and implementation.
  • Product, booking, or service information organized for customers.
  • Mobile-friendly pages and clear paths to contact or purchase.

Inventory application integration

Where the inventory system supports integration, the project can connect product or availability data to the website or store. The available interfaces, data quality, update frequency, and error handling are assessed before the integration scope is agreed.

Google Ads and Meta Ads account security

Account-security support can include reviewing who has access, authentication and recovery options, billing notifications, partner permissions, and campaign-change oversight. This service focuses on account and campaign security; it does not promise ad performance and is not paid-ad campaign management.

Clear scope before implementation

Website features, store checkout, inventory connections, hosting, support, and advertising-account work vary by business and platform. NYCConsult defines deliverables and dependencies with you before implementation.

Example projects

Small-business digital commerce starter packages

Choose a focused starting point or combine packages after checking platform fit and integration requirements.

Website and online store starter

Starting at $3,500 USD

  • Up to five core pages, responsive design, and a store with up to 20 products.
  • Standard checkout setup with one supported payment provider and basic on-page SEO.
  • Contact or purchase paths and a handoff for routine content updates.

Not included: custom inventory integration, extensive copywriting, paid apps, hosting, or ad spend.

Inventory integration starter

Starting at $2,100 USD

  • One inventory application connected to one store using an existing supported plugin or documented API.
  • One-way product or stock synchronization, a test plan, and basic error-handling guidance.

Not included: custom bidirectional middleware, data cleanup, or unsupported/undocumented systems.

Google Ads and Meta Ads security review

Starting at $350 USD

  • Review of up to one Google Ads account and one Meta Business portfolio.
  • Access, multi-factor authentication, partner permissions, recovery, and billing-notification checklist.
  • Written recommendations for reducing account-takeover and unauthorized-change risk.

Not included: campaign management, ad spend, guaranteed performance, or account recovery from an active compromise.

These indicative starting estimates apply the requested 30% reduction to internal planning baselines. They are not verified industry averages, prior published prices, or firm quotes. Final pricing follows discovery and an agreed scope; third-party fees, hosting, taxes, and out-of-scope work are additional.
