Kubernetes consulting

Operate Kubernetes with clear security and reliability practices

NYCConsult supports Kubernetes architecture and engineering for Amazon EKS, Azure AKS, and Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE), from cluster foundations through deployment and operational practices.

Review the whole platform, not just the cluster

Production readiness depends on the workloads, delivery process, identity model, network boundaries, observability, and team operating practices around Kubernetes. A review should identify the risks and operational tasks that matter for your services.

  • Cluster lifecycle, upgrades, capacity, and environment separation.
  • Workload identity, secrets handling, network policies, and admission controls.
  • Ingress, deployment strategy, GitOps, and rollback procedures.
  • Metrics, logs, traces, alerting, and service-level objectives.

Common engagement outputs

  • Architecture and security findings with prioritized recommendations.
  • Reference patterns for cluster and workload configuration.
  • Deployment, observability, and incident-response improvements.
  • Operational guidance for platform and application teams.

Choose managed services and tooling deliberately

EKS, AKS, and GKE each have distinct integrations and operating considerations. Recommendations should fit the cloud environment, workload needs, team skills, and existing platform standards.
