Cloud architecture consulting

Build a secure cloud foundation before workloads scale

NYCConsult helps teams plan cloud architecture and landing zones across AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. The work establishes clear foundations for identity, networking, governance, and operations.

When cloud architecture consulting helps

A landing zone is useful when an organization is starting in the cloud, expanding across accounts or subscriptions, preparing for regulated workloads, or trying to make infrastructure changes safer and more repeatable.

  • Multiple teams need consistent account, subscription, or project boundaries.
  • Security, networking, logging, or identity standards vary between environments.
  • Infrastructure needs reusable, reviewable provisioning patterns.
  • Cloud decisions need to support compliance and operational ownership.

Typical engagement deliverables

  • Current-state assessment and prioritized architecture decisions.
  • Account or subscription structure, network boundaries, and identity model.
  • Logging, encryption, policy-as-code, and security guardrails.
  • Infrastructure-as-code patterns, operating guidance, and implementation roadmap.

Designed around your cloud platform

Architecture choices depend on your workloads, existing technology, risk requirements, and team capabilities. The engagement can focus on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, or a multi-cloud environment without assuming that every organization needs the same reference design.
