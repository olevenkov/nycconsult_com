Cybersecurity assessment

Understand security risk and prioritize what to fix

NYCConsult provides cybersecurity evaluations and, when requested, authorized penetration testing within an agreed written scope. The goal is to give decision-makers clear findings and practical remediation recommendations.

Assessment and testing are scoped to your environment

Each engagement begins by agreeing on objectives, assets, permissions, timing, contacts, and boundaries. Testing is performed only with written authorization for systems the client owns or is authorized to assess.

  • Review cloud, application, identity, network, and security-control risks.
  • Agree the penetration-test scope, exclusions, timing, and escalation contacts.
  • Prioritize findings by risk, business context, and remediation effort.
  • Provide recommendations and a review of remediation options.

Clear deliverables

Deliverables can include an executive summary, technical findings, risk context, and a prioritized remediation roadmap. Any retesting or validation is agreed as part of the engagement scope.

What a penetration test does not provide

A penetration test is a point-in-time assessment, not a guarantee that every vulnerability has been found, proof of ongoing security, a compliance certification, or an audit opinion. Security decisions should consider the wider threat model, operating practices, and risk tolerance.
