Cloud FinOps consulting

Make cloud cost visible, explainable, and actionable

NYCConsult helps engineering and finance teams understand cloud usage, assign cost ownership, and evaluate optimization opportunities without treating a lower bill as the only measure of success.

Start with cost visibility and allocation

A useful FinOps review connects billing data to products, environments, and accountable teams. Where allocation is incomplete, tagging and account structure can be improved over time rather than requiring perfect data on day one.

  • Review account structure, tags, budgets, and cost allocation coverage.
  • Identify spend trends, anomalies, and major service categories.
  • Connect infrastructure cost to workload demand and business context.

Evaluate optimization opportunities

  • Rightsizing, autoscaling, and capacity planning.
  • Storage lifecycle, data transfer, and retention choices.
  • Pricing commitments and workload usage patterns.
  • Cost guardrails in infrastructure provisioning and delivery pipelines.

Keep optimization sustainable

Each recommendation should include an owner, expected trade-offs, and a way to verify the result. Reliability, performance, security, and engineering effort should be considered alongside spend.
